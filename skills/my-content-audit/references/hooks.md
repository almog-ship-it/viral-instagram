# Hook library

22 hook formulas. Used to **tag** hooks found in the wild (audit, research, trends) and to **write** new ones (viral-script). Always refer to a hook by its id.

Adapted from the 26 formulas in [instagram-agent-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill) by Jake Schincariol (MIT). Rewritten for Hebrew, merged where formulas overlap, Hebrew examples added.

## Rules for every hook
- Write the **spoken** line and the **on-screen** line separately. On-screen is shorter: 6 words or fewer. For a carousel, slide 1 headline = the on-screen line.
- No setup. Start at the sentence you would normally reach at second six.
- Say the number. "המון זמן" is not a number.
- If any other creator in the niche could say it, it is not this user's hook. Put in something only they can say.
- Deliver what the hook promised before the halfway point.

## Tagging hooks found in the wild
- Pick the closest id. If none fits, write `unclassified` and quote the line. Unclassified hooks are worth reading by hand: two of the same shape may be a formula missing from this list.
- Don't force a match. A podcast clip with no hook is `unclassified`, not H19.

## What a hook score can and cannot do
Jake Schincariol tested his hook scorer on 74 real hooks: it caught deliberately bad hooks well, but could **not** tell a creator's hits from the same creator's misses (close to a coin flip). Only concreteness (numbers, names) separated them. So: use the formulas and gates to **kill weak hooks**, never to promise which good hook will win. The user's own numbers after publishing decide that.
